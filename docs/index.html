Bottle Net

A free, open-source desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux, developed by Aung Khit Htoo.

Bottle Net helps you manage and publish videos from your own computer:

  • Collect and download public TikTok and Facebook videos.
  • Upload and schedule videos to your own YouTube channel and to Facebook Pages you manage. Set the title, description, tags, thumbnail and privacy, and pick a time.
  • Keep a calendar, an upload queue and a history of what was published where.

How Bottle Net uses your Google account

To publish to YouTube, click Connect YouTube in Bottle Net and sign in on Google's own page. Bottle Net never sees your password. It asks for:

PermissionWhy Bottle Net needs it
Manage your YouTube videos (upload)To upload the videos you choose to your channel and set their thumbnails.
View your YouTube accountTo show which channel is connected and check that the account has a channel before uploading.
Your email addressTo show which Google account is connected.

Bottle Net runs entirely on your computer. It has no server of its own: your videos, account details and sign-in tokens stay on your device and go only to Google (and Meta, if you connect Facebook). Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

You can disconnect at any time from the app's Accounts page or in your Google security settings. Details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Bottle Net's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Only publish what you may publish

Before you publish a downloaded video, Bottle Net asks you to confirm that you own it or have permission to publish it. Bottle Net uses YouTube API Services, and by using it with YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
